MSFS Airbus A330 MRTT

Airbus A330 MRTT brings multi-role tanker operations to Microsoft Flight Simulator with a refined exterior model, an A330-style virtual cockpit, and multiplayer-friendly in-flight refueling using boom and drogue animations. Includes French, RAF, RAAF, and Spanish variants plus configurable payload displays for formation and refuel scenarios, and requires the PMP Airbus A330 interior.
