This is an X-Plane 11 and X-Plane 10 scenery package for Coroa do Aviao Airfield (ICAO: SIFC, no IATA), which is a private airport for general aviation located in Iguarassu, Pernambuco, Brazil, north of Recife International Airport (SBRF). It includes corrected aprons, ramps, gates, taxiways, lighting system, city buildings (for Igarassu), ortho-photo scenery for the airfield area, and (XP11 only) airport ground services (like push back). SIFC Airfield is located next to the Island of Itamaraca, north of Recife International Airport (SBRF). This scenery was tested and is operational (as of June 24, 2017) in “X-Plane 11.02r1 64 bit”, Windows 10 Home. It was also tested and is also operational (as of June 24, 2017) in “X-Plane 10.51 64 bit”, Windows 10 Home. X-Plane 11 Installation: Two folders are provided. You only need the one marked XP11. Copy and paste the folder “BR PE SIFC – Coroa do Aviao Airfield XP11 v1.0.0” into your “X-Plane 11Custom Scenery” folder, as usual in the X-Plane world. Do not copy the folder marked XP10. X-Plane 10 Installation: Two folders are provided. You only need the one marked XP10. Copy and paste the folder “BR PE SIFC – Coroa do Aviao Airfield XP10 v1.0.0” into your “X-Plane 10Custom Scenery” folder, as usual in the X-Plane world. Do not copy the folder marked XP11. Additional scenery libraries needed: Only the default X-Plane 11 libraries, by Laminar Research, are needed. No third party libraries are used.
